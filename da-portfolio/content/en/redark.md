## A place for knowledge and personal projects

I started a technology blog to share what I learned and document what I built. It began with WordPress and my own PHP theme. Over time, maintaining the platform needed more and more attention.

## Why I moved away from WordPress

WordPress and its plugins required continuous updates and fixes and introduced additional attack vectors. I wanted to reduce those issues. In 2020, I migrated the live blog to React and Gatsby, removing WordPress entirely.

I moved the articles to MDX. The code is public. I chose an approach where I could keep content alongside the site’s code without continuing to manage the previous platform and its plugins.

## Migrating while keeping the URLs

Moving a live blog required care beyond the interface. I preserved the article URL structure and replaced absolute links with relative ones. I checked SEO repeatedly using several analysers.

Checking URLs and links was part of the migration, alongside moving the interface and content.

## What is live, and what remained an experiment

The live site uses Gatsby. I later built a Next.js version too, but it remained an experiment and did not replace the published blog. I link to it as a separate prototype.
